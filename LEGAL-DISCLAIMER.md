## Written authorization checklist

Before any practical test on a system you do not personally own, obtain written authorization that clearly identifies:

- The person or organization granting permission and their authority to do so.
- The exact systems, domains, IP addresses, applications, or networks in scope.
- The permitted testing methods and activities.
- The testing dates, times, and any operational restrictions.
- Prohibited actions, data-handling requirements, and emergency contacts.
- The process for reporting findings and stopping the test.

Keep a copy of the authorization and remain within its exact scope. Permission to test one system does not authorize testing connected systems or third-party services.
